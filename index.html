Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

Depending on the complexity, a standard business website takes 2-4 weeks, while custom web applications can take 6-12 weeks.

How much does it cost?

Every project is unique. We provide custom quotes after our discovery call and requirement analysis to ensure you only pay for exactly what your business needs.

Do you provide maintenance and support?

Yes, we believe in long-term partnerships. We offer ongoing maintenance, security updates, and performance monitoring for all our clients.

Can you redesign existing websites?

Absolutely. We can completely revamp your existing website to improve its design, performance, and conversion rates.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes, all our websites are built with technical SEO best practices. We also offer advanced SEO services to help you rank higher on Google.

Do I own the website once it's completed?

Yes, absolutely. Once the project is fully paid for and launched, you have 100% ownership of your website, including the design, content, and source code.

Will my website look good on mobile devices?

Definitely. Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices, so all our websites are built with a "mobile-first" approach to ensure flawless performance on any screen size.

What do you need from me to get started?

To begin, we'll need your business logo, brand colors, text content for the pages, and any specific features you want. Don't have these ready? We can help you create them.

Can I update the website myself after launch?

Yes! We build websites on user-friendly content management systems (CMS). We'll provide you with a dashboard and training so you can easily update text, images, and blogs without knowing how to code.

Do you provide domain and hosting services?

Yes, we offer complete end-to-end solutions. We can register your domain, set up premium lightning-fast hosting, and configure professional business emails for your team.
